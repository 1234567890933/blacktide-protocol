# Offline Tactical AI

40 actors, no server, no navmesh bake, 60 Hz near the player. This document is
the whole design, including the two bugs the headless tests caught.

## One state machine, four profiles

`Bot` extends the same `Actor` base as the player: same damage model, same
armour, same healing, same ballistics function. A bot is a player with a
different brain, which is the only way "the AI cheats" never becomes a topic.

```
DROP → ROTATE ⇄ LOOT
              ↓
        ENGAGE ⇄ COVER
           ↓  ↘
        FLANK  HEAL → ROTATE
```

| State | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| `LOOT` | Walk to the nearest item within 28 m; take it if it beats the current weapon tier. |
| `ROTATE` | Move to a random point inside the cordon, re-pathed every 3.5–7 s. |
| `ENGAGE` | Face the target, strafe (0.6–1.6 s direction flips), hold an ideal range (26 m snipers, 14 m everything else), fire in bursts. |
| `COVER` | When below 35% health and the profile likes cover, move to the best cover point between it and the threat. |
| `FLANK` | 70° off the target vector at 14–24 m, then re-engage from the new angle. |
| `HEAL` | Stop, apply a dressing, resume at 85% health. |

## Difficulty profiles

| | Reaction | Aim error | View range | Burst | Heal | Cover | Flank |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| RECRUIT | 0.62 s | 4.2° | 55 m | 3 | 35% | 35% | 10% |
| REGULAR | 0.38 s | 2.6° | 72 m | 5 | 60% | 60% | 30% |
| VETERAN | 0.24 s | 1.5° | 88 m | 7 | 80% | 80% | 50% |
| ELITE | 0.15 s | 0.9° | 105 m | 9 | 95% | 95% | 65% |

Aim error is **not constant**: it scales with distance (`0.6 + dist/90`) and
shrinks with the profile's `accuracy_growth` once the bot has been on target.
That is what produces the human pattern of "misses the first burst, then
suddenly does not".

## Perception

- 130° field of view, plus a 12 m "close" bubble where facing does not matter.
- Line of sight is a single raycast, excluding both the bot and the target.
- **Contact sharing:** a bot that sees an enemy writes it to the squad blackboard
  (`SquadManager.alert`). Teammates read it, so squads converge on a fight they
  did not personally see — pincer behaviour with no coordination code.
- **Hearing:** `receive_gunshot(pos, radius)` is available; suppressors shrink
  that radius via the attachment's `sound_scale`, so a quiet loadout actually
  keeps you off the map.

## Navigation without a navmesh

A 1200 m navmesh bake is expensive at match start and brittle around procedural
geometry. Instead:

1. **Two-probe steering** — cast 3.2 m ahead at 0°, ±28°, ±60° and take the first
   clear direction (`Bot._avoid`). The map builder keeps 3 m corridors between
   structures precisely so this works.
2. **Cover-point graph** — every structure registers its four corners into
   `MapBuilder.cover_points`; `nearest_cover(from, threat)` scores by distance
   minus alignment with the threat direction.
3. **Stuck detection** — if a bot moves less than 5 cm for 1.5 s while rotating
   or looting, it picks a random nearby waypoint.

## CPU budget

| Distance to player | Logic rate |
|---|---|
| < 70 m | every physics frame (60 Hz) |
| 70–160 m | 8 Hz |
| > 160 m | 3 Hz |

Throttled bots still apply gravity and `move_and_slide()` every frame; only
perception and decisions are throttled. Supporting caches:

- nearest-loot search cached for 0.6 s per bot,
- loot label proximity polled at 5 Hz instead of per frame,
- player interact scan at 20 Hz.

## Two bugs the headless tests caught (worth recording)

1. **Bots fired 2,898 shots and dealt 0 damage.** `Bot._ready` built a visual
   body but never called `setup_body()`, so every bot had *no collision capsule*.
   Raycasts passed straight through them. Fix: `setup_body(1.8, 0.36)`. The
   lesson: a bot without a collider still looks perfect on screen.
2. **Line-of-sight check blocked every shot.** The LOS ray excluded only the
   shooter, so it hit the *target* and reported "no line of sight". Fix: exclude
   both RIDs. The lesson: LOS means "nothing between us", not "nothing at all".

Both were found by `-- --smoke` printing per-bot counters (`fired`, `dmg`,
`los_fail`), which is why those counters are still in `bot.gd`.

## Match pacing

Measured with 40 operators on a 1.2 km island: ~20 alive at 90 s, ~14 at 4 min,
~9 at 7 min. Deaths are front-loaded because everyone starts inside the first
cordon; the shrink phases then do the work. To slow it further, lower
`GameState.settings["bot_count"]`, raise `DEPLOY_GRACE`, or grow `MAP_SIZE` —
all three are single-constant changes.
